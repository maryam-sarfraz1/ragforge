# Support

## Getting help

Email support@meridian.example, or use the in-app widget, which attaches your
organisation and recent request ids automatically and gets you a faster answer.
Enterprise customers also have a shared Slack Connect channel.

Include the `request_id` from any error response. It is the single most useful thing
in a ticket — with it we can find the exact trace in seconds; without it we are
guessing from timestamps.

## Severity and response times

| Severity | Definition                                      | Free      | Team    | Enterprise |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------|-----------|---------|------------|
| Critical | Production down or data at risk                 | —         | 8h      | 30 min     |
| High     | Major feature broken, no workaround             | —         | 1 day   | 2h         |
| Normal   | Broken with a workaround, or a bug              | community | 2 days  | 8h         |
| Low      | Question, documentation, feature request        | community | 4 days  | 1 day      |

Times are first response, not resolution, and are measured in business hours except
for Enterprise critical, which is 24×7. Free organisations are supported through the
community forum, which the team monitors on weekdays.

## Escalating

If a ticket has stalled, reply asking for escalation and it goes to a lead the same
day. Enterprise customers can page the on-call support engineer for a critical issue
via the escalation number in the shared channel — for production-down only.

## Status and uptime

Live status is at status.meridian.example; subscribe there for incident email. The
Enterprise SLA commits to 99.9% monthly uptime, with service credits of 10% of the
monthly fee below 99.9% and 25% below 99.0%. Claim within 30 days of the incident.
